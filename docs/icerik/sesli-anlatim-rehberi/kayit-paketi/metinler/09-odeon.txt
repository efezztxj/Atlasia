North of the State Agora, this small theatre-shaped building is known as the Odeon or Bouleuterion. Bouleuterion means a meeting place for the city council. The building may also have hosted performances. Stone seating, an orchestra area and a stage building give it the appearance of a small theatre. Inscriptions connect Vedius Antoninus and his family with the building in the second century, but evidence also points to an earlier council house. It is therefore better to think of these remains as a civic space renewed over time, rather than a building created once and never changed.
