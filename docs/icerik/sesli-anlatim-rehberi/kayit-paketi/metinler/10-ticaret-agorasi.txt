The Commercial Agora was a marketplace near the ancient harbour of Ephesus. Its almost square plan also gave it the name Tetragonos Agora. The area goes back to the Hellenistic period; expansion under Augustus and later alterations changed its appearance. Behind the colonnaded galleries surrounding the open courtyard were shops and workspaces. Gates connected the market to the city's streets. The nearby Library of Celsus and the Gate of Mazaeus and Mithridates help us understand this district within the ancient city. The map marker represents an approximate point, not the boundaries of the entire agora.
