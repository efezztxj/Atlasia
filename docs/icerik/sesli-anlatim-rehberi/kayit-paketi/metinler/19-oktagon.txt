The Octagon is an eight-sided funerary monument on Curetes Street, near the Hellenistic Heroon. For many years it was associated with Arsinoe, the sister of Cleopatra. However, a scientific study published in 2025 found that the examined remains belonged to a young male and could not be Arsinoe's. This result does not, on its own, explain every phase of the monument's use and burials.
