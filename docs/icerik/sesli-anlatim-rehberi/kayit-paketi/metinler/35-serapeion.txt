West of the Commercial Agora stands a large sanctuary commonly known as the Temple of Serapis, or the Serapeion. Built during the Roman period, its temple and surrounding architectural remains reflect the monumental character of ancient Ephesus. Despite the familiar name, it is debated whether the sanctuary was actually dedicated to Serapis. Scholars have interpreted inscriptions and finds in different ways and proposed other functions. That uncertainty matters when we look at the surviving remains. The map pin is an approximate centre of the area, not an entrance or an indication of access.
