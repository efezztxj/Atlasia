The Memmius Monument near Curetes Street is an honorary monument from the Roman period. Sources relate that Gaius Memmius commissioned it in connection with his family, and that the Roman dictator Sulla was his grandfather. Architectural fragments and figurative decoration can be seen among the remains. Reconstructions of its original appearance involve interpretation, so the present arrangement should not be treated as a certain image of the ancient building. The nearby Hydreion is a separate water-related structure. The marker approximately represents the area where the two remains stand close together.
