East of the Octagon and west of the Stoa of Alytarches, beneath the steps of Curetes Street, lies a small monument with a hexagonal plan. Uncovered in the 1994 excavation, it was almost completely buried when the street level was raised, so it is hard to pick out as a visitor. Its function is not certain; it is read together with the neighbouring upper-city monuments, the Octagon and the Heroon. Its name describes only its plan, not its identity. The position is approximated from the west-to-east sequence Heroon–Octagon–Hexagon; it is not a point measured on site. The photograph shows the street, not the monument.
