Across from the Great Theatre and north of Harbour Street stood the Theatre Gymnasium, a Roman complex combining exercise spaces and baths. Built in the second century, it was distinct from the Vedius Gymnasium north of the stadium. A colonnaded exercise court, known as a palaestra, formed part of the complex. The remains and scattered architectural fragments help us imagine how bathing and physical training shaped public life. Later changes and the unfinished state of excavation make some details uncertain. The map pin represents the broader complex, not an entrance. Check current access before visiting.
