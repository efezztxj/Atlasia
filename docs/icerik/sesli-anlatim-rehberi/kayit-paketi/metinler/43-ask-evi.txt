Near the Library of Celsus is a group of remains long called the House of Pleasure or brothel. The archaeological site sign explains that this was a mistaken interpretation, and describes private houses with courtyards and shops opening onto the street. The remains of the building complex help us understand domestic life in Roman Ephesus. This is not the same residential block as Terrace House Two. The map pin represents the wider area approximately, not an entrance or walking route.
