At the southwest corner of the State Agora stood a monumental water building known as the Fountain of Laecanius Bassus, or Hydrekdocheion. It was donated by the proconsul Gaius Laecanius Bassus around 80 CE. Ornate façades framed its rectangular pool on three sides, and water flowing from sculptures added movement to the architecture. It is distinct from another fountain along the southern side of the agora. The map pin approximately represents the structure, not an entrance or a route.
