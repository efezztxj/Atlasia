North of the State Agora stands the Prytaneion, an important place in the civic and religious life of Ephesus. It contained the city's sacred hearth. Priests known as the Curetes tended its fire, and their names were inscribed on the building's columns. Statues of Artemis of Ephesus found here during excavations are now displayed in the Ephesus Museum in Selçuk. The re-erected columns help us imagine the building's former appearance.
