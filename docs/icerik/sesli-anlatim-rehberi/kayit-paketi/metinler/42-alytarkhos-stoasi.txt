The so-called Alytarchs’ Stoa was a colonnaded hall on the south side of Curetes Street, in front of the Terrace Houses. Because of the slope it had two levels, with shops and workshops behind it. Surviving mosaics with geometric and plant designs recall the richness of this ancient street. Its name comes from an inscription referring to an official responsible for rules at athletic contests. The map pin represents the approximate centre of the long structure, not an entrance or safe walking route.
