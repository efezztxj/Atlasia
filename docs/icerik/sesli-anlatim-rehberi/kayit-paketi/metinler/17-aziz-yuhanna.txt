The Basilica of Saint John stands on Ayasuluk Hill in Selçuk. The remains visible today belong to a large Byzantine church built in the sixth century during the reign of Emperor Justinian. Christian tradition associates the site with the tomb of John the Apostle; this association should be understood as a tradition. The basilica lies in the Ayasuluk component of the Ephesus World Heritage site, separate from the ancient city core.
