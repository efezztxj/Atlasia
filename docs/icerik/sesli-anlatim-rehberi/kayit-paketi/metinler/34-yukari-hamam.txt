At the eastern end of the State Agora, near the Odeon, are the remains of the Upper Gymnasium and baths. Parts of this large Roman complex stood against the slope of Mount Pion. Baths were places not only for washing but also for meeting and social life. The complex has not been fully investigated, so questions about its plan and history remain. Some sources call it the Baths of Varius, a name also associated with the Baths of Scholastica on Curetes Street. These are distinct sites. The map pin represents the complex approximately; it is not an entrance.
