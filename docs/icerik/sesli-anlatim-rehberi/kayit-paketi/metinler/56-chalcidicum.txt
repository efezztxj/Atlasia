At the western edge of Domitian Square stands a massive substructure that carried the western end of the Basilica Stoa. Its name comes from the Latin *chalcidicum*, a porticoed hall added to a basilica. Built of large ashlar blocks, it opens onto the square through three chambers, and it is thought to have been added in the reign of Nero. The structure bore the weight of the stoa above while its front framed the public space. The mass itself survives; nothing of its decoration remains.
