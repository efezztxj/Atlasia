The Harbour Baths and Gymnasium formed a large Roman complex in the old harbour district of Ephesus, north of Harbour Street. Baths were combined with spaces for exercise and education. Its earliest phases belong to the Roman imperial period; the complex was later damaged and repaired at different times. The nearby Halls of Verulanus and their broad court were associated with athletic and public life in this area. Only part of the remains has been excavated. The map pin marks the approximate centre of the wider complex, not a visitor entrance or a safe walking route. Check current access before visiting.
