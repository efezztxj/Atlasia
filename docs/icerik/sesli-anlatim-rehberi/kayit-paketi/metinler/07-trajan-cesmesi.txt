The Fountain of Trajan stood on Curetes Street as a monumental display of the city's water supply. An inscription records that the fountain was dedicated to Artemis and Emperor Trajan by the benefactor Tiberius Claudius Aristion and his wife. The basin was framed by an ornate, two-storey façade. A colossal statue of the emperor once stood above the water. Today surviving architectural fragments help us imagine how water, sculpture and civic generosity came together in the public life of Ephesus.
