Moda is the headland of Kadıköy reaching into the sea. Early in the morning people walk the shore, walk their dogs, buy a simit and sit down on the pier. The tea gardens stay open all day; the best table is the one with the wind at your back and the Princes' Islands in front of you. At weekends residents clean the shoreline together; here the coast is not a view but a shared room. In the evening, as the sun sets, the stones are still warm and people sit watching the water. Moda's story is not in monuments but in these habits: neighbours meeting in the same place at the same hour.
