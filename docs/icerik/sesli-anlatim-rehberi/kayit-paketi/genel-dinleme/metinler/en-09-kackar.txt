The Kaçkar Mountains are the back garden of the eastern Black Sea. High pastures, glacier lakes and mist arrive together, and the highest point reaches almost four thousand metres. In summer, herding still goes on in the yaylas around Ayder; wooden houses, hay barns and meadows keep the same rhythm. July and August are the best months to camp beside the glacier lakes; in the evening the mist closes the view and in the morning it opens again with the sun. Rain here is not a surprise, it is the rule. Paths fade in places, so trust the waymarks and the shepherds.
